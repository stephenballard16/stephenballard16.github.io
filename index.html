Fantasy Football Automation

Fantasy Football Automation is a personal, non-commercial tool used by a single individual to manage their own ESPN fantasy football team.

Each week, it saves a fantasy football data workbook and weekly report into a folder it creates in the owner's own Google Drive, and emails the report to the owner.

The app is not offered to the public and does not collect data from anyone other than its owner.
