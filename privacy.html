Privacy Policy

App: Fantasy Football Automation
Effective date: October 8, 2026

Who uses this app

This is a personal, non-commercial app used only by its owner. It is not available to the public.

What Google data it accesses

The app uses the Google Drive drive.file permission. It can access only the files and folders it creates itself. It cannot see, read, or change any other files in Google Drive.

How the data is used

The app creates folders and saves fantasy football workbooks and reports in the owner's Google Drive. No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and security

Access credentials are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private automation environment and are never published. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from their Google Account permissions page.
